Delete your BillInsight account

You can delete your account and all of its data at any time.

Option 1: in the app (immediate)

  1. Open BillInsight and sign in.
  2. Go to Profile → Account.
  3. Tap Delete account and confirm.

Your account and data are deleted right away, and you are signed out.

Option 2: by email (without the app)

Email soumyamaheshwari1234@gmail.com from the email address you use to sign in, with the subject "Delete my BillInsight account".

We may reply to confirm the request comes from you. We delete the account within 30 days and email you when it's done.

What gets deleted

  • Your account and sign-in details
  • All bills and receipts, their items, and payment details
  • Scan history, including text extracted from your bills
  • Your saved address or location
  • Registered devices and notification tokens
  • Your preferences and processing history
  • The app's offline copy of your bills on your device (when you delete from the app)

What may be kept for a limited time

  • Database backups: may contain your data for a short period until they expire.
  • Crash reports: deleted automatically under our crash-reporting provider's retention period.
  • Emails you sent us, such as feedback or this deletion request: kept in our inbox until we delete them.

Uninstalling the app does not delete your account. Use one of the options above.
