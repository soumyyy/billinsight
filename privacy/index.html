Privacy Policy

Effective 14 November 2026

BillInsight ("the app", "we", "us") helps you find bills and receipts in your photos and keep an organized record of your spending. This policy explains what data the app collects, why, who processes it, and how you can delete it.

Summary

  • Your photo library is scanned on your device. Only photos that look like bills or receipts, and photos you choose to add, are uploaded.
  • We use your data only to run BillInsight. We do not sell it, and we do not use it for advertising.
  • All data is sent over encrypted connections (HTTPS).
  • You can delete your account and data at any time, from the app or by email.

Photo library access

If you allow photo access, BillInsight checks your photos on your device with a built-in model to find likely bills and receipts. Photos that do not look like bills never leave your device. You can scan manually, or turn on background scanning to check new photos automatically. You can change photo access or turn off background scanning at any time.

Data kept on your device

The app keeps an offline copy of your bills and its scan progress on your device so it works without a connection. This copy is removed when you delete your account.

How bill images are processed

An uploaded image is sent to our server and then to Google's Gemini API, which reads the text and structure of the bill. We keep the extracted bill details. The uploaded image is deleted from our server once processing finishes. A copy of the extracted text is kept in your scan history until you delete your account.

Service providers

We share data only with providers that help us run the app, and only for that purpose:

  • Clerk: sign-in and account management
  • Google (Gemini API): reading bill images
  • Neon: database hosting
  • Our cloud hosting provider: runs the BillInsight server
  • Expo and Google Firebase Cloud Messaging: delivering push notifications
  • Sentry: crash reporting and diagnostics

We may also disclose data if the law requires it.

Security

All traffic between the app and our services uses HTTPS. Access to your data requires your signed-in account. No system is perfectly secure, but we work to protect your data and keep only what the app needs.

Retention and deletion

We keep your data while your account exists. When you delete your account, we delete your account and everything stored with it: bills, items, payment details, scan history, saved address, devices and preferences. Feedback emails stay in our inbox until we delete them. Crash reports are deleted automatically by Sentry under its retention period. Data may also stay in encrypted database backups for a short period until those backups expire.

See Delete your account for how to do it.

Your choices and rights

  • View and edit your bills and profile in the app.
  • Turn off notifications, photo access or background scanning in the app or in your device settings.
  • Delete your account and data at any time.
  • Email us to ask for a copy of your data, a correction, or deletion. Depending on where you live, you may have more rights under local law, such as India's Digital Personal Data Protection Act or the GDPR.

Children

BillInsight is not intended for children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect data from them. If you believe a child has given us data, contact us and we will delete it.

Changes

We will update this page when the policy changes and change the effective date above. For significant changes, we will also notify you in the app.
